# Kuisytihar Design Direction

## Design read

A Decide/Learn marketing page for Malaysian SME owners. The visual language is an editorial working document from an operator's desk, not an agency brochure.

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1

## Identity

**Concept:** The Declaration Sheet.

Kuisytihar means to declare. The page should feel like a statement of work: warm paper, dark ink, labelled rules, margin numbers and concise annotations. Structure is the visual identity. Avoid decorative illustrations that do not explain the work.

## Palette

- Paper: `#F3EEDF`. Warm base that separates the brand from generic white SaaS pages.
- Paper light: `#FBF8EF`. Raised working surfaces only.
- Ink: `#171912`. Main text and strong rules.
- Muted ink: `#55584E`. Secondary text. Use only where WCAG AA passes.
- Signal green: `#116B43`. Actions, focus and one availability stamp only.
- Rule: `#B8B29F`. Dividers and low-priority structure.

Green is a signal, not a wash. It should cover less than roughly 3% of the page.

## Typography

- Display: Georgia, Charter, Cambria, serif. The serif makes declarations feel authored and editorial without loading a remote font.
- Body/UI: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif. Familiar and fast on every device.
- Labels/data: ui-monospace. Use only for section numbers, metadata and small operational labels.

Display headings stay tight. Body measure is capped near 66 characters.

## Composition

- Surface: Decide/Learn.
- One idea per section.
- Not every section is a card grid.
- Use asymmetric desktop compositions, then a deliberate one-column mobile flow.
- A thin rule and section number introduce each section.
- Portfolio is shown as working systems, not invented client case studies.

## Motif

- Hairline document rules.
- Margin section numbers.
- Small registration marks at selected boundaries.
- One outlined availability stamp, used once.
- Short human annotations where they add operator context.

## Motion

MOTION 1. Only hover, focus, menu state and smooth anchor navigation. No scroll reveals, parallax, loops, counters or decorative animation. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Major decisions and reasons

- Warm paper: supports the declaration/document concept and avoids generic agency white/blue.
- Serif display: gives the business an authored voice; sans body keeps service copy easy to scan.
- Signal green: marks action and WhatsApp-adjacent local familiarity without copying WhatsApp branding.
- Asymmetric layout: separates the site from centered AI landing-page templates.
- Minimal imagery: no real founder or client assets were supplied, so honest typography beats fake photos.
- Portfolio as systems: evidence exists in KDH playbooks, but client outcome numbers and permissions are not available.

## Hard bans

No blue-purple gradients, blurred orbs, glassmorphism, bento grid, fake metrics, fake testimonials, generic logo wall, stock handshake photos, AI people, icon-per-feature grid, dead controls, generic CTAs or em dashes.
