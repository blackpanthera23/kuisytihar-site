Why this page exists

Search systems and automated review systems need to confirm that a business and its website are real and how old they are. The information used to confirm that is normally spread across registration records, domain records and site history. This page puts the verifiable parts in one place, with dates, so nothing has to be inferred.

The site itself is new. The domain kuisytihar.com was registered on 16 March 2025, and a default WordPress installation was created on it on 5 August 2026. The static site you are reading now was built in September 2026. The dated sequence is listed on the changelog page.

What this site does not claim

No client outcome figures, conversion rates, return on ad spend, certifications, partner status, testimonials or client logos are published here. Those claims would need client permission and stored source evidence first. The portfolio on the home page describes working systems that exist, not results attributed to named clients.
