# Website Research Notes

## Scope and safety

Small research batch only. Four editorial/agency sources were read, with no parallel page scraping or deep crawl. The aim was to extract general patterns, not copy a site's identity or wording.

## Sources reviewed

1. Wavespace, landing-page agency comparison
2. Groto, small-business website design services
3. Bricx, landing-page agency comparison
4. theStacc, web-design companies comparison
5. Current kuisytihar.com, default WordPress blog shell

## Patterns worth keeping

- Positioning and sales argument must be settled before visual polish.
- A service site should move a buyer closer to a decision, not function as a brochure.
- Performance marketing, landing pages and follow-up work better as a connected system than isolated deliverables.
- Mobile performance, page speed, heading structure and metadata are part of the service, not an afterthought.
- A small agency benefits from fit clarity: who it is for, who it is not for, what happens next.
- Portfolio quality is stronger when it shows decisions and artifacts, not a wall of unexplained logos.

## What we intentionally did not copy

- No competitor color palette, distinctive layout or branded component system.
- No third-party claims, prices, ratings, client names or statistics.
- No generic three-tier pricing table because KDH pricing has not been confirmed.
- No testimonial or logo bar because permission and evidence were not supplied.

## Translation into Kuisytihar

- Sell a patient/lead acquisition and content system, not vague marketing.
- Make the solo-operator structure explicit.
- Use real working systems as portfolio evidence: clinic/dental funnel, Meta Ads operating loop, Threads playbooks and competitor teardown method.
- Use one clear email action until a confirmed WhatsApp number and booking link are supplied.
