# Kuisytihar Content and Portfolio Guide

## What the site may claim now

- Kuisytihar is solo-operated.
- Services: Paid Lead Generation, Threads Account Management, Growth Audit & Consultation.
- Working systems exist for clinic/dental acquisition, Threads content operations, competitor funnel teardown and Meta Ads decisions.
- Akmal is the direct operator and point of contact.

## What the site must not claim without evidence

- Client counts, ROI, ROAS, conversion uplift or lead volume.
- Meta/Google partner status or certifications.
- Testimonials, customer logos or named case studies without permission.
- Prices, guarantees or availability counts that Akmal has not confirmed.

## How to add a portfolio item

Every item needs:

1. A real context or business category.
2. The problem as supplied by the client/source.
3. The artifact or work performed.
4. A screenshot or diagram with sensitive details redacted.
5. A result only when the client permits it and the source is stored.
6. A source note in the repo, not visible secrets.

If item 5 is unavailable, show the work and decision logic. Do not imply an outcome.

## Copy voice

- First-person singular: saya, not kami.
- Natural BM-English code-switching for familiar marketing terms.
- Short, specific sentences.
- Explain what happens next.
- No corporate ceremony or inflated promises.
